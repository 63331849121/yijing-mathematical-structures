This preprint studies linked multiplicative orders in the norm-one subgroup of a binary quartic extension. Genus-one norm fibers give effective joint-generation and double full-order densities. Uniform estimates over every nonzero relative trace are completed by the classification of all empty slices: the subgroups of orders 9 and 11 at t=8 and t=32. The counts also determine scalar realizations of affine complete mappings and their prescribed companions.

This revision proves complete support on prescribed primitive inner norms for every s>=2, including composite B=t^2+1. The proof combines a three-coordinate prime sieve with primitive b, an infinite tail bound, exact integer controls, and finite witnesses covering every remaining norm orbit. New distribution results give an exact Kloosterman formula and unsieved variance asymptotic to t. For odd s, the cubic coordinate of the second-norm sieve is a genus-two trace bounded by 4 sqrt(t); a projective Frobenius-spectrum criterion excludes all relevant rank-one Lang twists and gives a mixed second-moment bound. The corresponding full-order pointwise error coefficient is 8M-12.

The mixed-moment constant is a fixed conductor integer and is not numerically evaluated. The full double full-order variance bound retains its W(A)^2 factor; no asymptotic main term for that variance is claimed. Classical Kloosterman monodromy, Dickson substitution, Artin-Schreier and trace-formula methods are explicitly credited.
