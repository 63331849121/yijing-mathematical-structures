Paper A / Paper N identity and authorship, 2 November 2026

Paper A: An affine Singer layer on F2^6, its (T,D,K) triple, and
<T,D> = AGL(1,64). Sole author: Baohua Li.
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22731492. This focused earlier note remains unchanged.

Paper N: Norm-fiber densities and relative traces in characteristic two.
Authors, in order: Wencong Huang; Baohua Li.
Concept DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23721669.
Wencong Huang joined the development of this later norm-fiber article.
The v17 and v21 norm-fiber records belong to Paper N, although their older
repository paths and tags used paperA. Their creator metadata is corrected
to the two authors; this correction does not apply to the original Singer note.

The current canonical repository directory is PaperN_norm_fibers/.
PaperA_norm_fibers/ is a compatibility path for the norm-fiber article,
not the original Singer note. Historical DOI files, dates, checksums and
tags keep their published bytes and identifiers. The new tag uses paperN.
The formal titles of both works are retained and their DOI chains stay distinct.
