Norm-fiber densities and relative traces in characteristic two
Baohua Li -- v21 editorial revision, 29 October 2026

We study linked multiplicative orders in a quartic norm-one subgroup C_d in characteristic two, with t=2^s, s>=2, and d=(t+1)(t^2+1). Let R_d consist of a in C_d for which 1+a is in C_d and the two elements jointly generate C_d; let D_d count a for which both elements have order d. Genus-one norm fibers give effective error estimates in this growing-index family. Exact averaging yields |R_d|=(t+1)^2 product_(p|d)(1-p^(-2))+O(t^(3/2)2^omega(d)) and D_d~(phi(d)/d)^2(t+1)^2 as s tends to infinity, with a separate explicit error for the double full-order count. For every nonzero relative trace c in F_(t^2), the order-d population with a+a^(t^2)=c has main term (phi(d)/d)(t+1) and uniform error O((phi(d)/d)2^omega(d)sqrt(t)). The empty nonzero slices are exactly the subgroup of order 9 at s=3 and the subgroup of order 11 at s=5. For every s>=2 the trace-one classification and two off-slice certificates prove the existence of a with ord(a)=ord(1+a)=d. These counts determine scalar realizations of affine companion groups. General index bounds and a norm obstruction give an optimal uniform index exponent of 1/4, with a logarithmic gap in the sufficient range.
