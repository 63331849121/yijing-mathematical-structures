NEW RATIONAL AND EXACT-ORDER INTERFACE
RelativeTraceTransfer.lean: 34 proofs, 10 definitions/abbreviations.
For t=2^s, A=t+1, B=t^2+1, d=A*B, s>=1 and a nonzero c fixed by the t^2-power
Frobenius, the module defines actual field functions a=c/(1+gamma) and
gamma=(a+c)/a. traceEquiv is an actual Equiv between the admissible root sets.
Zero denominators are excluded from root/support membership, not assumed away.
The two inverse identities, a^(t^2)=a+c, a^B=w^-1 and a^(t^2-1)=gamma are
proved by characteristic-two field arithmetic. The coprimality relations
Coprime(A,B) and Coprime(B,t-1) are derived from the binary parameter.

order_components proves order(a)=d iff order(gamma)=B and order(w)=A under
the proved root and power identities. The forward direction uses the exact
order of powers and the t-1 coprimality; the reverse uses divisibility of
order(a) by A and B and its divisibility into d. The finite-set cardinality
bijection is explicit, not a supplied assumption. M_eq_parameter_count and
M_eq_two_mul_support_count transfer the a population to the already proved
two-preimage support count. Weight-shift and Frobenius identities give the
M_(hu) formula. The zero slice is proved empty by order(a) dividing t^2-1.

NAMED ALGEBRA.NORM / ALGEBRA.TRACE INTERFACE
NamedNormTraceTransfer.lean: 13 proofs, 2 definitions.
Works with actual finite fields K,E,L, compatible algebra maps and scalar
tower, card K=2^s, [E:K]=[L:E]=2. The finite extension degrees and cardinality
are the manuscript's structural hypotheses, not the desired counting result.
The quartic degree, card E=t^2, norm powers and quadratic trace formula are
derived using mathlib's finite-field norm/trace formulas. The count is defined
by Algebra.norm K a=1, orderOf a=d, Algebra.trace E L a=c. It is proved equal
to the power/root population, then to the parameter/support populations.

In particular named_M_shifted_parameter_count and named_M_shifted_support_count
prove, for h in K nonzero and u in E with Algebra.norm K u=1:
  M_(h*u) = filtered gamma count
          = 2 * #{z in T_h : order(z*u^(-2))=t+1},
with all scalar embeddings explicit in the Lean statement. The right support
is the same normalized reciprocal-trace support used by the earlier modules.
The zero slice and zero-count iff empty-support criterion are also proved.
These are structural cardinality identities; complete numerical evaluation
or classification of those populations is not claimed.

WHAT HAS CLOSED SINCE V16
The original a/gamma bijection, exact-order component equivalence, named
norm/trace identification, and M_c count transfer are now formalized. They
must no longer be listed as remaining gaps for the stated finite-field tower.
The code does not disguise an assumed population bijection as a count theorem.
The local statements use s>=1 and therefore cover the paper's s>=2 domain.

REMAINING LIMITS
1. Concrete polynomial field models, original JSON arithmetic, chosen
   certificate generators and the coordinate change to the arbitrary cyclic
   generator have not been linked to Lean. Finite support and polynomial
   certificates remain independently checked exact-computation inputs.
2. The complete one-prime/prime-kernel structural branches and exact quotient
   fiber/exceptional-zero counts are not all connected. The unique c=h*u
   decomposition and the final C9/C11 case assembly are not formalized.
3. The genus-one descent, Lang characters, conductor/weight arguments,
   4*sqrt(t)/8*sqrt(t) bounds, prime sieve, integer tail and all-parameter
   assembly are not part of this build. Neither full Theorem 5.1 nor full
   Theorem 5.4, nor the entire manuscript, is claimed formally verified.
4. The universal field-tower theorems assume the genuine finite fields and
   their compatible degree-two extensions; this is not a checked construction
   of each concrete binary polynomial representation used in the data logs.
