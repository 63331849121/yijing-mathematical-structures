**Baohua Li**

Quadratic orbit maps and three-Engel Lie algebras in characteristic two.

This consolidated revision presents the exact ordinary Lie correspondence and two-sided quadratic coordinates, the prescribed derived-square obstruction and exact central repair, sharp first-ascent costs, and the quadratic/cubic boundary. It includes selected equality endpoints and one subsequent covering-type obstruction. Full parameter classification and coefficient-ring 18s theory are deferred. Costs are relative to specified bottoms; no global class-six optimum or strict R dimension penalty is claimed.

The revision clarifies classical predecessors, retains e(d)-1 <= kappa(d) <= e(d), removes a superseded conditional result, and explicitly identifies E's published Theorem 4.4 as the input to a separate metabelian dimension comparison. Core proofs are independent of new companion results.
