# Four-zero tail-palindromic polynomials over F2

**Reciprocal obstructions and quadratic-transform intersections**  
Baohua Li · Research note · 12 pages · 29 October 2026

The note completely classifies the intersections of the four-zero
tail-palindromic family with full-degree binary quadratic rational
transformations. The translated Q branch has two infinite shape families
and three degree-14 exceptions; the translation-invariant branch has four
reducible outputs at degrees 14 and 30. A uniform translation obstruction
also applies to any even-degree tail palindrome with a multiple of four
missing tail coefficients. The reciprocal common divisor and the exact
number of surviving position pairs provide a complementary arithmetic screen.

Proposition 3.5 gives an explicit descent polynomial for the remaining B
family and its uniform proof. The general primitive-existence conjecture
C4 remains open. Classical transformation and irreducibility results are
credited at the points where they are used.

The complete arithmetic records cover 161700 candidates at 98 even degrees
6–200, including 2663 primitive polynomials. First witnesses cover all 148
even degrees 6–300. Degrees 202–300 are first-witness prefix checks.
The data archive supplies inputs, candidate verdicts, exact-order residues,
factorization records, and structural verification programs with outputs.
