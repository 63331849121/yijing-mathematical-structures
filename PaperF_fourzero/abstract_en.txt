We study monic even-degree polynomials over F2 whose nonleading coefficients form a palindrome with constant term one and exactly four zeros. We classify their intersections with full-degree quadratic rational outputs and exclude iterated doubling from an even input degree. An exact reciprocal common divisor gives an arithmetic screen and a closed count of surviving position pairs. For the polynomial obtained by cubing all roots, counted with multiplicity, we prove that the tail is never palindromic. The corresponding quintic image is tail-palindromic exactly for one degree-10 exception or an explicit arithmetic-progression family; its number of missing tail coefficients is always 2 modulo 4. Sparse norm identities and independently checkable finite parameter certificates prove these root-power classifications for all degrees. They exclude degree-preserving cube- and fifth-root extraction within the four-zero family. Reproducible computations identify 2663 primitive polynomials at the 98 even degrees from 6 to 200 and verify first witnesses through degree 300. The primitive-existence conjecture C_4 remains open.
