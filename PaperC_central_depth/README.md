## Mathematical scope

We study how the central depth of the second derived ideal controls nilpotency class in ordinary three-Engel Lie algebras over characteristic-two fields with more than two elements. We determine the exact centre-by-metabelian class law max(5, d + 2), complete top central covers and rank-uniform stable ranges through sixth central depth. Fifth-depth stability starts optimally at rank nine; sixth-depth stability holds from rank ten. The rank-seven and rank-eight boundaries carry distinct cubic representations, including a nonsplit Frobenius structure. The least center dimension realizing the prescribed free metabelian quotient is ceil((d + 1)/2), attained by a three-Engel construction. Symbolic propagation is separated from exact finite starting certificates. This independently readable 28-page preprint develops the ordinary central-depth part of Paper G.
