## Results and scope

For the entire ordinary three-Engel Lie class in characteristic two,
|k|>2, every stable starting rank a_s exists and has linear order:
s+2^floor(log2(s-1)) <= a_s <= 7s+2^ceil(log2(s))-9 <=9s-11 (s>=3).
The general upper proof uses a fixed-anchor Frobenius-jet cone and
all three mixed critical profiles; current constructions give the lower
bound. The first complete-bottom lifting delay R(d) also has linear order.
The revision retains a5=9, a6=10, R(9)=5 and R(10)=6; gives uniform
determinant-twisted symmetric-cube quotients; and proves exact minimum
center formulas for the free metabelian and graph quotients.

The arbitrary-depth proof uses no new finite start. Sixteen bounded
replay tasks passed. The unchanged extended endpoint systems retain
their earlier replay scope. Optimal constants, exact general a_s,
the remaining cubic kernel and optimal unrestricted e(d) remain open.
