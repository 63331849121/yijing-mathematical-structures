# Paper A and Paper N: identity, authorship and version history

The labels distinguish two works with separate titles and DOI chains.

| Work | Formal title | Author(s), in order | DOI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper A | An affine Singer layer on F2^6, its (T,D,K) triple, and <T,D> = AGL(1,64) | Baohua Li only | [10.5281/zenodo.22731492](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22731492) |
| Paper N | Norm-fiber densities and relative traces in characteristic two | Wencong Huang; Baohua Li | [All versions: 10.5281/zenodo.23721669](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23721669) |

Paper A is the original focused note of approximately ten pages. The later
expanded norm-fiber work is Paper N. Wencong Huang joined at the Paper N stage;
he is not added to the earlier single-author note. The current Paper N
introduction credits Li's note as the origin of the affine companion construction.

## Earlier norm-fiber editions

The following already had the Norm-fiber title and belong to Paper N, despite
the old use of Paper A in their repository paths and release names:

- v17, 28 October 2026: [10.5281/zenodo.23721670](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23721670).
- v21, 29 October 2026: [10.5281/zenodo.23757428](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23757428).
- Current general revision, 2 November 2026: [10.5281/zenodo.23880175](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23880175), archive edition v22.

The v17/v21 creator metadata is corrected to Huang, Wencong; Li, Baohua.
Their deposited PDFs and source archives retain their original bytes and bylines,
with a dated explanation in the record metadata. Their DOI, dates and historical
Git tags are not rewritten. These metadata corrections concern the norm-fiber
article only; the original affine Singer note stays single-author.

## Current paths and citation

- `PaperN_norm_fibers/` is the canonical current directory.
- `PaperA_norm_fibers/` is a compatibility path for the formerly named norm-fiber
  article, not the original ten-page Singer note.
- `PaperA_singer_TDK.pdf` and `.tex`, and `CITATION_SINGER_NOTE.cff`, identify the
  earlier single-author Paper A.
- New norm-fiber tags use `paperN`. Older `paperA` tags remain historical identifiers.
- The version label v22 is for archive management and is omitted from the formal title.

Use the appropriate formal title and DOI when citing either work. Do not combine
their author lists or move the original Singer DOI into the norm-fiber version chain.
