# Sharp ideal growth of metabelian restricted Lie algebras

Baohua Li. Preprint, 34 pages. Updated 1 November 2026.

Manuscript: [PaperS.pdf](PaperS.pdf).
Editable LaTeX and exact verification: [PaperS_draft_and_source.zip](PaperS_draft_and_source.zip).

Sharp fixed-rank restricted ideal growth, typical quotient layers, Hall partition laws and the rank-two whole-quotient nilpotence rate. Fine probability laws use uniform ideals.

The companion paper is independently developed: G retains the structural interfaces and a summary of S; S supplies the full ideal-growth and probability proofs.
