## Paper A and Paper N: distinct identity and authorship

Paper A is the earlier focused affine Singer note, solely by Baohua Li,
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22731492. Its title, sole authorship and files are preserved.

Paper N is Norm-fiber densities and relative traces in characteristic two,
by Wencong Huang and Baohua Li, in that order. Huang joined at the norm-fiber
article stage. Its concept DOI is 10.5281/zenodo.23721669. The v17/v21
norm-fiber records have corrected two-author metadata; that correction does
not apply to the earlier Singer note. Historical tags and file bytes are kept.
The current canonical directory is PaperN_norm_fibers/. The old
PaperA_norm_fibers/ path is retained for compatibility with earlier naming.
See PAPER_IDENTITIES.md and PaperN_norm_fibers/AUTHORS_CORRECTION.txt.
