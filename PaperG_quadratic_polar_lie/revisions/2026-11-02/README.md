## Mathematical scope

This 209-page research collection studies finite binary quadratic maps satisfying f composed with f = f + id. It develops global polar nilpotence, ordinary Lie reconstruction, exact metabelian dimension and population laws, central-depth bounds, and geometric and observation applications. The final additions identify the precise ordinary three-Engel image with a sextic identity, establish uniform biquadratic coordinates and a sharp cubic encoding, and describe compact central obstructions. This version is preserved as the frozen unified collection. Three independently readable manuscripts develop its structural, extremal-enumerative and ordinary central-depth themes: Papers Q, E and C.
